The quasi-Riemann hypothesis concerns regions in the complex plane where the Riemann zeta function has no zeros. A mathematical manuscript presents an argument for a zero-free half-plane with real part greater than seven eighths. The stated result also covers Dirichlet L-functions and certain finite-order Hecke L-functions, while allowing the principal pole at one. This is a result about a zero-free region, not a proof of the full Riemann hypothesis.
